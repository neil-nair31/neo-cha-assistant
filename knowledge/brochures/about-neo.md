# About Neo Logistics

Neo Logistics is a private partnership **Licensed Customs Broker (CHA)** formed in **2007**. It is the **flagship company of Neo Group**.

Neo provides logistics support across:
- Shipping
- Freight forwarding
- Transportation
- Warehousing
- Console agent services
- Steamer / vessel agency
- Stevedoring

Over 15+ years, Neo has become one of the leading licensed customs brokers at **Port Cochin (Kochi)**, with operations also in **Chennai**.

## Scale
- Handles approximately **8,000–10,000 containers annually**
- Also handles break-bulk cargo and inland transportation, including over-dimensional cargo
- Neo Group has turnover of over **INR 1,000 million** (~₹100 Cr)

## Group companies
- **Neo Logistics (2007)** — Licensed CHA, transportation, warehousing, specialized mining transportation; flagship of Neo Group
- **Neo Trades (2004)** — Import–export trading in raw cashew and iron ore from Odisha (Orissa); iron ore licence issued by Dy. Director Mines / Mining Officer, Koira, Odisha

## Vision
Growth through sustained logistics excellence and end-to-end supply chain solutions for clients, with continuous upgrading against logistics challenges and total quality control.

## Mission
Provide unique and innovative supply chain solutions, ensure timely delivery of service and materials, uphold environmental and safety awareness, and build sustainable long-term mutual relationships.

## Values highlighted by clients
Transparency, dedicated teams, on-time delivery mindset, strong logistics expertise, honesty (no false commitments), and cost-conscious ownership mentality.

## Public case study — JSW Steel Ltd, Salem (5PL / multimodal)
Neo successfully executed complex multi-mode movement for JSW Steel Ltd, Salem:
1. Road: Salem → ICD Coimbatore (~200 km)
2. Rail: ICD Coimbatore → Port Cochin
3. Sea: Port Cochin → Port Mundra, Gujarat
4. Rail: Mundra → nearest rail head to destination
5. Road: last-mile delivery

Multiple handlings occurred at each stage. Despite weather-related sailing delay (force majeure), the movement was completed successfully.
