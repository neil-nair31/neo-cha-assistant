## Industries served (https://www.neologistics.org/core-industries/)
With almost 20 years of transportation experience, Neo develops shipping, storage, distribution and value-added solutions customized for:

1. **Industrial raw materials** — help manufacturers reduce inventory and streamline transport of industrial raw materials
2. **Chemicals** — special handling and storage care for complex chemical products to prevent safety hazards
3. **Automobiles** — dedicated supply chain management for the automobile industry (including LCVs mentioned in About)
4. **Mining** — logistics keyed to mine location and supply-chain complexity; iron ore transport and rake handling
5. **Textiles** — expertise across textile transportation stages
6. **Agro products** — storage and material flow from production sources to destination (cashew; aquatic products such as shrimps/prawns mentioned in About)
7. **Sanitary wares** — careful handling of washbasins, water closets, bath tubs, etc.

Also mentioned in About narrative: **Steel**, **Cement**, agro/cashew, aquatic products, automobiles (LCVs).
