# Industry playbooks (Neo Logistics)

Neo has ~20 years of transportation experience with customised shipping, storage, distribution and value-added solutions.

## Steel, cement & industrial raw materials
- Inventory-aware transport planning for manufacturers
- Break-bulk and containerised inbound raw materials
- Multimodal corridors (see JSW Salem case: road–rail–sea–rail–road)
- Neo handles ODC (over-dimensional cargo) with planning

**Client should provide:** material specs, weight/dimensions, origin, plant destination, required arrival window (not guaranteed in chat).

## Chemicals
- Handling and storage with safety-first procedures for complex chemical SKUs
- DG / haz documentation must be reviewed before acceptance
- Neo coordinates clearance + bonded/warehouse legs where scoped

## Automobiles & LCVs
- Dedicated supply-chain design for automotive flows
- Documentation for chassis, CKD/SKD where applicable

## Mining & iron ore
- Mine-to-destination logistics including **rake loading/unloading**
- Iron ore transport from mines across India
- Complexity scales with mine location and multimodal legs

## Textiles
- End-to-end textile logistics across production and port legs
- Time-sensitive flows for mills and exporters

## Agro products (cashew, shrimp, prawns, etc.)
- Cold-chain / hygiene awareness for aquatic exports
- Storage and material flow from farm/ processor to port/factory
- CFC Cashews (Kollam) is a public reference client

## Sanitaryware
- Careful handling of fragile SKU mix (washbasins, WC, bathtubs)
- Packaging and stowage discipline to reduce damage claims

## When the assistant should escalate
- DG / haz without SDS
- Novel commodity first-time import
- Project cargo >20 TEU or break-bulk specials
- Any request for duty %, freight rate, or guaranteed clearance date
