# Scenario FAQs (approved guidance)

## I am a first-time importer to India via Cochin. What happens?
1. Engage Neo (contact form / KYC / email customercare@neologistics.org)
2. Share proforma invoice, packing list, BL draft, IEC, GSTIN
3. Neo CHA reviews HS chapter, PGA needs (FSSAI, BIS, PQ, etc.)
4. Pre-arrival BOE planning; duty payment coordination after assessment
5. Examination if RMS selects; out-of-charge; DO; transport/warehouse as contracted

## I export cashew from Kerala. Can Neo help?
Yes — agro is a core industry. Neo publicly serves CFC Cashews (Kollam) for import/export clearance and transport. Share commodity grade, bags vs containers, destination port, and shipping bill supporting docs.

## Can Neo clear at Chennai if my factory is in Tamil Nadu?
Neo maintains a Chennai operations office (Egmore) with dedicated email docschennai@neologistics.org. Confirm shipment profile with Neo for Chennai port corridor work.

## Do you handle LCL / console cargo?
Neo offers **Console Agent** services — cargo consigned to overseas counterparts at destination. Scope and partners confirmed per lane.

## Can you move cargo Cochin to Mundra or other ports?
Neo executed sea leg Cochin→Mundra in JSW multimodal case. Lane feasibility, carrier, and scope require Neo ops review — not auto-promised in chat.

## What is your AEO benefit for my imports?
Neo holds **AEO–LO**. Programme-level benefits exist for trusted logistics operators under Indian Customs AEO framework. Applicability to a specific shipment depends on current CBIC circulars and your importer profile — Neo team confirms.

## I need warehousing near port before my factory picks up
Neo publicly offers warehousing with organised storage. Duration, location (Cochin/Chennai), and charges require Neo quotation.

## How do I register as a Neo client?
Public 3-step: (1) contact/KYC (2) service contract (3) start shipping. KYC form: /know-your-customer/

## Can you tell me total landed cost?
No in chat. Landed cost = goods value + freight + insurance + duties/taxes + local charges. Neo quotes after documents and classification review.

## Someone on WhatsApp sent me a duty rate screenshot — is it correct?
Do not rely on screenshots. Duty depends on HS, notifications, exemptions, and valuation. Route to Neo CHA with invoice description and technical literature.

## I want to speak to a human
Offer escalation. Collect name, company, email/phone with consent. Cochin: customercare@neologistics.org | Chennai: docschennai@neologistics.org | Mon–Sat 9:30–17:30.
