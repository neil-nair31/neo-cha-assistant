# FAQs — Approved General Answers

Answers below are for first-line guidance. They must not invent Neo pricing, exact duties, or binding timelines.

## Q: What does Neo Logistics do?
Neo is a licensed Customs House Agent / Customs Broker (CHA) and logistics company (Neo Group flagship, since 2007). Services include customs brokerage, freight forwarding, transportation, warehousing, steamer/vessel agency, stevedoring, console agency, multimodal transport, and related shipping support — mainly at Cochin and Chennai, with broader multimodal capability.

## Q: Are you AEO certified?
Yes — Neo publicly presents **AEO–LO** authorization and AEO branding. AEO-LO is the logistics-operator track under India’s AEO programme. Specific facilitation benefits for a given shipment should be confirmed with Neo operations against current CBIC rules and the shipment profile.

Cochin is Neo’s primary CHA stronghold; Chennai is an operations branch. Clients have publicly referenced interest in Chennai and Krishnapatnam expansion, and Neo has executed multimodal work involving Mundra. Confirm port-specific coverage with Neo for each enquiry.

## Q: How many containers do you handle?
Public materials state roughly **8,000–10,000 containers per year**, plus break-bulk and inland / ODC moves.

## Q: Can you handle chemicals / steel / textiles / cashew / vehicles?
Yes — these industries are listed on Neo’s industries and About pages. Hazardous / DG cargo needs correct declarations, packaging, and approvals; Neo’s team must review documents before committing.

## Q: How do I get a quote?
Share commodity, approx volume (TEU/weight), origin & destination, Incoterms if known, and preferred timeline. Neo’s sales / ops team will quote after reviewing details. **This assistant never states prices.**

## Q: What documents are needed for import clearance (general)?
Typically (varies by commodity and scheme):
- Commercial invoice & packing list
- Bill of Lading / Sea Waybill (or Air Waybill)
- Import licence / authorisations if applicable
- Certificate of Origin if preferential / required
- Insurance certificate if required under Incoterms
- IEC, GSTIN of importer
- Product-specific certificates (FSSAI, CDSCO, BIS, PQ, AQ, etc. when applicable)

Neo’s CHA team will confirm the exact checklist for your commodity.

## Q: What documents are needed for export clearance (general)?
Typically:
- Commercial invoice & packing list
- Shipping instructions
- GST / LUT / drawback / RoDTEP related details as applicable
- IEC, AD code / bank details where needed
- Quality / inspection / phytosanitary certificates as applicable
- Letter of credit documents if under LC

## Q: Can you tell me the exact customs duty?
**No exact percentage should be stated as fact** without formal classification and current tariff/notifications. Duty depends on precise HS code, notifications, exemptions, IGST, social welfare surcharge, anti-dumping, etc. Neo’s team will advise after reviewing product details and classification.

## Q: How long does clearance take?
There is **no guaranteed public SLA**. Time depends on documentation completeness, RMS facilitation, examination, PGA NOCs, port congestion, and payment. Neo aims for fast, accurate clearance but must not promise dates in chat.

## Q: Do you offer warehousing and transportation after clearance?
Yes — Neo publicly offers warehousing and transportation alongside CHA.
