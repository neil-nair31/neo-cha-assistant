# Incoterms & charge ownership (educational)

**Cite as:** ICC Incoterms® general concepts (not legal advice).

## Common terms importers mention
| Term | Plain meaning | Typical customs clearance payer |
|------|---------------|--------------------------------|
| EXW | Buyer collects at seller premises | Buyer/importer arranges all |
| FOB | Seller delivers on board at port | Buyer handles main carriage + import clearance |
| CIF/CIP | Seller pays freight + insurance to named place | Buyer still import-clears in India |
| DAP | Delivered at place — seller bears to destination | Import clearance usually buyer unless DDP |
| DDP | Seller delivers duty-paid | Seller must be able to import-clear — rare for casual sellers |

## Local charges (names only — **no rates in chat**)
- THC, documentation fee, seal, weighment
- CFS handling, storage, ground rent
- CHA agency fee, agency DO charges
- Transportation, tolls, detention/demurrage if delayed
