# Citable Rules Corpus — India (Public Educational)

**How to cite:** When answering from this file, name the source header (e.g. “Per CBIC AEO programme framing (Circular 33/2016-Customs as commonly referenced)…”).  
**Never invent circular numbers.** Prefer general programme facts; for live notification text, direct users to CBIC / ICEGATE and Neo’s team.

## 1. ICEGATE
**Source framing:** Indian Customs Electronic Gateway (ICEGATE) — national customs EDI / trade portal.  
- Used for electronic filing of Bills of Entry, Shipping Bills, IGM/EGM and related messages.  
- ICEGATE provides FAQs and a trade portal chatbot for portal how-to questions.  
- Official portal: https://www.icegate.gov.in/  
**Assistant rule:** Explain concepts; do not claim live filing status from this chat.

## 2. Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) — India
**Source framing:** CBIC AEO Programme (widely referenced via Circular No. 33/2016-Customs and later amendments; official portal aeoindia.gov.in).  
Key public points:
- AEO is a trust-based trade facilitation programme aligned with WCO SAFE Framework concepts.
- **Importers/Exporters:** tiers AEO-T1, AEO-T2, AEO-T3.
- **Other supply-chain operators** (customs brokers, logistics providers, custodians, warehouse operators, terminal operators, etc.): **AEO-LO**.
- Benefits (programme-level, check current circular for exact entitlements): facilitation in processing/clearance, deferred duty (where eligible), DPD/DPE type benefits for eligible AEOs, reduced BG in some cases, etc.
- Neo publicly holds **AEO–LO**.

## 3. Customs Broker / CHA regulation (high level)
**Source framing:** Customs Brokers Licensing Regulations (CBLR) framework under Indian Customs.  
- A CHA/customs broker acts on behalf of importers/exporters for customs work under licence.  
- AEO-LO status can interact with broker licence validity/facilitation per current CBIC circulars (confirm live rule before asserting duration numbers).  
**Assistant rule:** Describe role; do not invent licence numbers or legal opinions.

## 4. Classification & tariff
**Source framing:** Customs Tariff Act / Harmonized System (HS) as adopted in India’s Customs Tariff.  
- Duty liability depends on **correct HS classification** + applicable notifications (exemptions, ADD, QR codes schemes, etc.).  
- Self-assessment places responsibility primarily with the importer/exporter; CHA assists.  
**Guardrail:** Never state “your duty is X%” without Neo specialist review.

## 5. Valuation
**Source framing:** Customs Valuation Rules (transaction value primary method).  
- Assessable value generally based on transaction value with statutory additions (freight, insurance, assists, royalties, etc. when applicable).  
- Related-party / royalty cases may need deeper review.

## 6. IGST on imports
**Source framing:** GST law as applied to imports.  
- Imports typically attract IGST (and compensation cess where applicable) in addition to basic customs duty stack.  
- Input tax credit / reverse charge nuances are case-specific — do not advise accounting treatment in chat.

## 7. Preferential origin / COO
**Source framing:** Preferential trade agreements & Certificate of Origin rules.  
- Preferential BCD rates require valid origin evidence under the relevant FTA/PTA.  
- Neo site references Certificate of Origin under customs notifications media.

## 8. Multimodal Transport Operator (MTO)
**Source framing:** Multimodal Transportation of Goods Act / registration framework (India).  
- MTOs assume responsibility for multimodal contracts end-to-end under applicable terms.  
- Neo displays **MTO** certification publicly.

## 9. MSME
**Source framing:** Udyam / MSME registration framework.  
- Neo displays MSME certification on the public site. This is a business status display, not a customs duty exemption by itself.

## 10. Common Indian EXIM identifiers
- **IEC** — Importer Exporter Code (DGFT)
- **GSTIN** — Goods & Services Tax Identification Number
- **AD Code** — Authorized Dealer bank code for remittances / export proceeds
- **PAN** — Permanent Account Number

## 11. Product-group approvals (examples only)
Depending on commodity, Indian imports/exports may need PGA clearances such as:
- FSSAI (food)
- CDSCO (drugs/devices)
- Plant Quarantine / Animal Quarantine
- BIS / QCOs
- Explosives / petroleum / chemical controls

Always verify commodity-specific rules with Neo + relevant authority.
