# Worldwide Customs & Trade Frameworks (Educational)

> Purpose: help Neo’s assistant answer **general** international logistics / customs questions that importers ask.  
> This is **not** country-specific legal advice. For operational moves, Neo must confirm origin/destination rules case-by-case.

## WCO — World Customs Organization
**Cite as:** WCO public framework concepts  
- Harmonized System (HS) nomenclature for classifying goods worldwide  
- SAFE Framework of Standards — basis for many national AEO programmes  
- Revised Kyoto Convention — modernizing customs procedures (high level)

## Incoterms® (ICC)
**Cite as:** ICC Incoterms® (general)  
- Define seller/buyer obligations for delivery, risk, and cost allocation (not ownership transfer alone).  
- Common: EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP.  
- Incoterms do **not** replace customs clearance responsibility nuances — DDP especially needs careful local import capability.

## Bill of Lading fundamentals
**Cite as:** general maritime practice  
- Negotiable BL vs Sea Waybill  
- Clean vs claused  
- Originals / telex release / express release  
- Consignee / notify party accuracy critical for DO

## Container types (general)
20' / 40' / 40'HC / 45', reefer, open top, flat rack, tank, OOG — choose by cargo nature and port equipment.

## Dangerous Goods
**Cite as:** IMDG Code (sea), IATA DGR (air) concepts  
- Correct UN number, class, packing group, labels, declarations mandatory  
- Misdeclaration is high risk — Neo must review documents before acceptance

## US trade (high-level educational)
**Cite as:** general CBP / ISF concepts  
- Importer Security Filing (ISF “10+2”) for ocean imports to US  
- Entry filing via ACE ecosystem  
Neo may coordinate international freight legs; US clearance often needs a US customs broker of record unless scoped otherwise.

## EU trade (high-level educational)
**Cite as:** general Union Customs Code concepts  
- EORI numbers  
- Import declaration (H1 etc. families) via national systems  
- AEO programme in EU (different from India’s AEO tiers)

## Middle East / GCC (high-level)
Free zones, destination customs, and document legalization (attestation/embassy) may apply — confirm per country.

## ASEAN / Asia (high-level)
FTA COO under RCEP / bilateral FTAs can reduce duty when rules of origin met — documentation discipline is critical.

## Phytosanitary / fumigation (cross-border)
**Cite as:** general ISPM-15 (wood packaging) & destination fumigation rules  
Neo site mentions Australian fumigation among media topics — destination agriculture rules vary and can block clearance if ignored.
