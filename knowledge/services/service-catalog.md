# Neo Logistics Service Catalog

Neo designs, controls, and manages supply chains with professional end-to-end expertise. All descriptions below are public marketing descriptions from Neo — they are informational, not quotes or contracts.

## 1. Licensed Customs Broker (CHA)
Ensures imported and exported items meet all regulations, packing standards, laws, and statutory requirements. Neo performs customs clearance documentation and coordination at ports where Neo operates, especially Cochin and Chennai.

Typical CHA activities (general industry process — Neo confirms case-by-case):
- Document collection and scrutiny (invoice, packing list, BL/AWB, COO, licences, etc.)
- Filing bills of entry / shipping bills via ICEGATE where applicable
- Classification support coordination with the client’s trade team
- Assessment / examination liaison with Customs
- Duty payment coordination as instructed by the client
- Out-of-charge / LEO follow-up
- Delivery / dispatch coordination after clearance

**Guardrail:** Neo does not publish fixed rates here. Any pricing, duty %, or timeline must be referred to the Neo team after reviewing documents and HS classification.

## 2. AEO–LO Authorization
Neo holds **AEO–LO** authorization. Benefits are offered because Neo meets compliance criteria and supply chain security standards under India’s Authorized Economic Operator programme for logistics operators (customs brokers / related logistics entities).

What AEO–LO generally means for clients (public programme framing):
- Trusted trader / logistics partner status recognition by Indian Customs
- Facilitation benefits associated with an AEO logistics operator
- Stronger compliance and supply-chain security posture

Clients should ask Neo’s team which facilitation benefits apply to a specific shipment (varies by port, scheme, and current CBIC circulars).

## 3. Transportation
Effective, well-organised shipment options tailored end-to-end. Neo has established alliances for transportation via **sea, road, and rail**.

Includes inland moves, port–factory corridors, and specialized cargo handling as scoped by Neo operations.

## 4. Steamer / Vessel Agent
Services connected with the ship’s husbandry or dispatch performed effectively — port and vessel-related agency support as contracted.

## 5. Stevedore Agent
Cargo operations (loading / unloading) performed with professional care and expertise.

## 6. Freight Forwarding
Coordinates shipment of goods from the seller’s premises to the buyer’s specified location — documentation, carrier booking coordination, and milestone follow-ups as agreed.

## 7. Warehousing
Scientific, organised storage to maintain goods’ value and quality; temporary / transit warehousing linked to clearance and distribution where Neo provides it.

## 8. Multimodal Transport Operator (MTO)
Multimodal transport contracts taken in charge with responsibility and liability under applicable MTO arrangements. Neo is shown as an **MTO**-certified entity on its public site.

## 9. Console Agent
Cargo consigned to respective overseas counterparts at the destination of consignee — LCL / console agency coordination.

## 10. Shipping Line related operations
Shipping operations from load port to discharge port managed competently where Neo is engaged in that capacity.

## How to start shipping with Neo (public 3-step guide)
1. **Register** — Fill the contact / KYC form; Neo’s onboarding team contacts you
2. **Service contract** — Agree to terms and sign the contract
3. **Start shipping** — Hand over goods from the preferred location; Neo handles agreed scope

## Onboarding / KYC (Know Your Customer)
Neo collects KYC for client onboarding, typically including:
- Company name, constitution, date of establishment, address
- PAN, GST, IEC (for consignee/shipper)
- CHA / MTO number if applicant is a broker/forwarder
- Directors / partners contact details
- Authorised person + accounts/finance contacts
- Bank details
- Uploads: IEC, GST certificate, utility bill, PAN

Submit via https://www.neologistics.org/know-your-customer/
