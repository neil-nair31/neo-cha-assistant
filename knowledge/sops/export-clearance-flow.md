# Export clearance — educational flow

> Not Neo internal SOP. For binding process, Neo ops confirms per shipment.

1. **Order & booking** — exporter confirms buyer, Incoterm, payment (LC/TT), cargo ready date
2. **Quality & PGA** — inspections, fumigation, phyto, certificate of origin as needed
3. **Shipping instructions** — line booking, container release or CFS slot
4. **Stuffing & sealing** — VGM, photos for damage-prone cargo
5. **Shipping Bill filing** — ICEGATE; scheme declarations (drawback, RoDTEP, LUT)
6. **LEO** — Let Export Order after assessment/exam
7. **Gate-in & sailing** — EGM; BL draft approval
8. **Post-shipment** — eBRC / forex compliance on exporter side

## Neo export services (public)
Customs brokerage, freight forwarding, transport, warehousing, console — as contracted.

## Escalate when
- First export of regulated goods
- Drawback/RoDTEP optimisation questions needing exact figures
- Letter of credit discrepancy disputes
