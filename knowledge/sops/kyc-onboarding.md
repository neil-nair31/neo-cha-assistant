# KYC & onboarding (public)

## Purpose
Neo collects KYC before providing CHA / logistics services to new clients.

## Typical fields (from public KYC page)
- Company name, date of establishment, constitution (Pvt/Ltd/Partnership/Proprietorship)
- Address, PAN, GST, CHA/MTO number (if broker/forwarder), IEC (if consignee/shipper)
- Nature of business
- Directors/partners contacts
- Authorised signatory + accounts/finance head contacts
- Bank name, account, IFSC
- Uploads: IEC, GST certificate, utility bill, PAN (self-attested)

## Declaration
Client declares information true; agrees to notify changes and re-submit documents.

## After KYC
1. Service contract / terms agreed
2. SOP handover for documentation templates
3. Live shipments under agreed scope

## Assistant behaviour
- Encourage KYC for new clients wanting ongoing service
- Do not collect bank/PAN in chat without consent — direct to secure KYC form
