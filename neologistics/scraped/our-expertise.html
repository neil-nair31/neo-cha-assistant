Our Expertise

Neo logistics is an entity that designs, controls, and manages your company’s supply chain with Professional expertise. We ensure that we provide fast, accurate, and quality service on your end to end needs.

Customs Brokerage

All the imported and exported items meet all the regulations, packing, laws and other requirements.

Warehouse facilities

Storing goods in a scientific and organised manner to maintain their value and quality.

Transportation

Effective and well organised shipment options tailor-made from end to end

Freight forwarding

The shipment of goods from the seller’s space to the buyer’s specified location is fulfilled with coordination.

Vessel Agent

Services in connection with the ship’s husbandry or dispatch is performed effectively

Stevedore

Console Agent
